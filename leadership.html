Leadership

The People Behind The Precision

Decades of combined leadership driving Vocto Technologies' vision across India and Europe.

Managing Director

Radhakrishnan R

Managing Director, Vocto Technologies

With over 30 years of experience in electronics and photonics manufacturing, Radhakrishnan R has built Vocto Technologies from the ground up into a globally connected engineering services partner, earning the trust of leading OEMs and technology companies across aerospace, defence, healthcare, and industrial sectors.

He leads with a philosophy of uncompromising quality and operational discipline - ensuring that every engagement, from early-stage design through to volume production, is delivered with the precision and accountability that demanding global markets require.

Honorary Chairman

Dr. Heikki J. Sipilä

M.Sc. (Tech.) 1969 · D.Sc. (Tech.) 1978, Helsinki University of Technology, Department of Technical Physics

A globally recognised pioneer in X-ray and gamma detector technology with over five decades of experience, Dr. Sipilä originated the portable X-MET X-ray fluorescence analyser and the Courier 30 continuous XRF analyser at Outokumpu Oy, led development of high-resolution proportional counters at Outokumpu Electronics, and initiated space instrument development at Outokumpu Instruments.

He co-founded Metorex International Oy and Baltic Scientific Instruments, leading space instrument programmes with detectors deployed in scientific satellites across Europe, the USA, and Japan. A Member of the Finnish Academy of Technology and founder of Fenno-Aurum Oy, he brings decades of scientific leadership and detector expertise to Vocto Technologies in an honorary, non-operational capacity.

Director

V Meenakshi Sundaram

Director, Vocto Technologies

With deep expertise in engineering operations and manufacturing processes, he plays a pivotal role in overseeing the company's production capabilities, quality systems, and technical strategy - ensuring that every product delivered meets the exacting standards of Vocto's global clientele.

A hands-on approach to engineering leadership and a commitment to continuous improvement have been instrumental in scaling the company's manufacturing capacity while maintaining the precision that defines the Vocto brand.
