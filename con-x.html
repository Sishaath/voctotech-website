Product Overview

Continuous On-belt Analysis - No Sampling Required

The CON-X is mounted directly above the conveyor belt and continuously identifies the elemental composition of the material passing beneath it. Unlike laboratory analysis, there is no delay, no sample preparation, and no human intervention - results are delivered in real time for immediate process decisions.

Mounted above conveyor - analyses material in motion, no belt stoppage
Detects all elements from Al (Z=13) to U (Z=92)
Stable results in harsh environments: dust, extreme temperatures, variable humidity
Results independent of lump size and humidity within the allowable range
OPC, 4–20 mA and Ethernet outputs for easy integration
Remote monitoring and support via Internet
Why CON-X

Key Features

Engineered for the most demanding industrial environments in mining, mineral processing, and heavy industry.

Real-Time On-line Analysis

Continuous measurement while the belt is running. No delays, no lab turnaround, no sampling queues. Data delivered immediately to your control room.

Non-Destructive

XRF technology measures elemental composition without altering or consuming the material. Full production throughput is maintained during analysis.

Broad Element Detection

Detects and quantifies elements from Aluminium (Z=13) through Uranium (Z=92) - covering virtually every element of industrial importance in mining and processing.

Rugged Industrial Design

Built for dust, low/high temperatures, and variable humidity. High precision and stability of results is maintained even in the most severe plant environments.

Simple Operation & Integration

Easy operator interface. OPC communication, 4–20 mA analogue output, and Ethernet connectivity allow seamless integration with existing plant DCS or SCADA systems.

Remote Support

BSI provides remote diagnostics and support via Internet. Firmware updates, calibration assistance, and troubleshooting without requiring an on-site visit.
