On-line Conveyor XRF Analyzer

Real-Time Elemental Analysis. Directly on the Conveyor.

CON-X provides continuous, non-contact and non-nuclear elemental analysis of material moving on a conveyor belt, helping mining and mineral-processing operations monitor grade variation and make more informed blending, beneficiation, and process-control decisions.

Overview

Better Visibility Into Material Grade

Laboratory analysis remains important for calibration and verification, but sampling provides results for selected samples at specific points in time. Material composition can vary between samples. CON-X adds continuous on-belt measurement to give plant teams more frequent information about the material moving through their process.

Continuous On-Belt Measurement

Monitor elemental composition of material moving on the conveyor, rather than relying only on intermittent sample results.

More Timely Process Information

Use ongoing grade information to support blending, beneficiation, and operational decisions.

Designed for Plant Integration

Measurement data can be integrated with plant control and monitoring systems using supported interfaces, subject to project configuration.

Non-Nuclear

CON-X is a non-nuclear device, without the burden of nuclear licensing, safety and high costs.

CON-X complements laboratory analysis; it does not remove the need for appropriate calibration and verification.

How It Works

How CON-X Works

The CON-X on-line conveyor XRF analyzer measures characteristic X-ray fluorescence from material moving on a conveyor belt. The system processes the detected signals to provide elemental measurement results over a configured measurement period.

  1. Measure

    The analyzer head is installed above or across the conveyor to measure the moving material without direct contact.

  2. Analyze

    The detector measures characteristic X-ray fluorescence signals associated with elements in the material.

  3. Use the Results

    Elemental results and configured averages can support grade monitoring, process decisions, historical trending, and plant-system integration.

Applications

Applications Across Mining and Mineral Processing

CON-X can be configured for suitable applications where elemental composition of material moving on a conveyor needs to be monitored. Application suitability depends on the material matrix, target elements, installation conditions, calibration, and measurement requirements.

Iron Ore

Monitor elemental composition to support grade awareness and process decisions across iron-ore handling and processing.

Chromite and Ferrochrome

Support elemental monitoring for chromite ore, ferrochrome, and related processing streams.

Manganese Ore

Track relevant elemental composition in manganese-ore processing applications.

Base-Metal Ores

Evaluate suitable applications involving copper, zinc, and other base-metal ores.

Industrial Sand

Assess selected impurity-monitoring applications, including iron content in industrial sand.

Slag and Metallurgical Materials

Evaluate suitable applications for slag and other metallurgical bulk materials.

Technical Details

Technical Overview

CON-X system configuration is selected according to the material, target elements, conveyor arrangement, measurement objectives, and plant environment.

Detector
Silicon Drift Detector (SDD)
Excitation source
Sealed X-ray tube; application-dependent
Element range
Sulphur (Z=16) through uranium (Z=92), subject to application and measurement conditions
Measurement
Continuous acquisition with configurable measurement periods and rolling averages
Installation
Over-belt or cross-belt configuration, depending on site requirements
Plant interfaces
Modbus TCP/RTU and OPC-UA, subject to configuration
Calibration
Calibration using customer reference samples and appropriate standards
Environmental conditions
Operating temperature and ingress protection per current datasheet
