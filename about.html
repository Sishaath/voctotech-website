Our Story

30 Years of Building Visions

Founded in 1995 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Vocto Technologies - formerly known as Dakshin Speaker Manufacturing Pvt Ltd - began as a specialist electronics manufacturing unit serving the rapidly growing Indian technology sector. From our earliest days, precision and reliability were non-negotiable - principles that remain central to everything we do today.

In the early 2000s, recognising the transformative potential of photonics, we expanded our capabilities to include advanced optical and photonics sub-assemblies. This move positioned us uniquely as a manufacturer capable of serving the most demanding segments - aerospace, defence, and scientific instrumentation.

In 2018, we established our European presence through our operations in Finland, through our Finland office, serving as the bridge between our Indian manufacturing capabilities and our growing European client base, combining cost-efficient production with European quality expectations.

In 2024, we rebranded from Dakshin Speaker Manufacturing Pvt Ltd to Vocto Technologies Pvt Ltd - marking a new chapter in our evolution as a comprehensive electronics and photonics engineering company, reflecting the breadth of our capabilities and our ambitions for the decades ahead.

Since 1995

Our Journey

Thirty years of milestones that have defined who we are and shaped the precision engineering we deliver today.

1995

Founded in Chennai

Vocto Technologies established as a precision electronics manufacturing unit in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, with a focus on reliability and quality for technology clients.

Early 2000s

Photonics Expansion

Expanded manufacturing capabilities to include advanced photonics sub-assembly and optical alignment, enabling entry into high-precision scientific and industrial markets.

2008

MEPZ-SEZ Certification

Achieved Madras Export Processing Zone (MEPZ) Special Economic Zone certification, enabling seamless export operations and unlocking access to global OEM clients.

2018

European Operations Established

Launched our European operations in Finland establishing local presence and support for European OEMs and technology companies.

2024

Rebranded as Vocto Technologies Pvt Ltd

Formerly Dakshin Speaker Manufacturing Pvt Ltd, the company rebranded to Vocto Technologies Pvt Ltd - reflecting three decades of evolution from electronics manufacturing into a full-spectrum engineering and photonics company.

2025

MoU with a Leading State University

Signed a Memorandum of Understanding with a leading state university in Tamil Nadu for joint research and development collaboration - combining academic expertise with our manufacturing capabilities to accelerate innovation in emerging technology fields.

2026

Cloud & Research Expansion

Launched our Cloud Services division (IaaS & SaaS) for engineering clients, while deepening neuromorphic computing research, positioning Vocto for the next decade of growth.
